# What Immvela can honestly claim (checked against the repo, 2026-10-03)
LIVE (present tense OK):
- Documents: reads the Energieausweis and the Grundbuchauszug; stores many other document kinds;
  checks documents against each other and flags contradictions; flags expiring certificates.
  Extraction is a DRAFT the agent confirms; nothing read is used until confirmed. Never "100%".
- Every confirmed value records who confirmed it and which document it came from. Listings, facts
  and documents belong to the OFFICE, not the individual agent. (Each person has their own login.)
- Publish hold: an ad missing required energy values is held, for every agent in the office.
  Publishing always asks for approval.
- Quill: Exposé, brochure and social captions drafted from confirmed values, in the German of the
  listing's country (AT/DE/CH). Office brand on documents.
- Vignette: AI staging, every result labelled as virtually staged.
- Verlag: posts to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube from one place, after approval.
- CRM import: listings come in from an OpenImmo export (onOffice, Justimmo, Propstack, FLOWFACT).
- Document checklist per listing: SNS's standard lists (AT flat, AT house, DE flat).
- German first, English available. Closed beta.
NEXT (label "Next", never dates, never present tense):
- Enquiries: answers and qualifies enquiries (never books viewings or quotes prices).
- Office rules: the office sets required documents, templates, and manager approval before publishing.
- An owner overview of what every agent confirmed and published.
- Portal publishing (willhaben, ImmoScout24). Walkthrough video from a phone sweep.
- EU-only processing.
NEVER CLAIM: "GDPR compliant", "EU-hosted", "regulated", "compliant ads", "legally safe",
"no training on your data", any accuracy number, customers, testimonials, pricing.
DATA FACTS (for the Trust page): documents and text are processed by AI providers based in the USA;
staging photos by a separate AI image provider; per-office data separation enforced in the database;
account deletion on request; documents are kept as evidence and not deleted on the photo schedule;
Immvela never states a legal retention period. Unknown, show as visible placeholders:
hosting region, the list of service providers, the data processing agreement (AVV) availability.
