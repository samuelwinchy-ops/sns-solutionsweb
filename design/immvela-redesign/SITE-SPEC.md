# Immvela.com home page: the spec (agreed with Samuel, 2026-10-03)

## Rules
1. One claim, one proof, per screen: headline (the claim) + ONE visual (the proof) + at most ONE sentence.
   A section that needs two visuals is two sections, or one moves to a subpage.
2. Layered disclosure: the home page makes claims; Modules and Why carry detail.
3. Muse rhythm: text one side, product the other, alternating sides. Plain headlines, grey subtext.
4. One primary action on every screen: Apply for the closed beta. Sign in stays small in the nav.
5. Strict scale: five type sizes, two weights (400, 600), 8pt spacing grid, same section padding everywhere.
6. Less but better: before adding anything, name what it replaces.
7. Five-second test: the hero alone must say what Immvela is and who it is for.
8. Motion budget: the hero sequence is the ONLY autoplay. Everything else is still; motion only when
   the visitor acts (tap a value, drag staging).
9. Honesty: describe only what works today. Auto understands "get it ready" and a fixed set of
   requests; free-sentence understanding and reasoning aloud are NOT built, so never present them.
   No pricing, roadmap, testimonials, or unbacked numbers.
